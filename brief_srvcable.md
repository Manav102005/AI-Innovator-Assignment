# Investment Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a leading manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors, catering primarily to power utilities and data center developers. In Q1 FY27, the company reported a revenue of ₹1,428 crore, reflecting a 35% year-on-year increase, while net profit rose 6% to ₹82 crore. However, operating margins contracted to 11.2% due to a 14% rise in copper prices. The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of June 30, 2026.

## Bull Case
- **Strong Revenue Growth:** The company maintains a revenue growth guidance of 15-17% for FY27, supported by robust demand from power transmission and data center projects, which are expected to double their contribution to domestic revenue in two years `[Source 2]`.
- **Capacity Expansion:** The new Bharuch plant is on track to commence production in Q3 FY27, expected to increase cable capacity by 30%, enhancing the company's ability to meet growing demand `[Source 3]`.
- **Improved Export Contribution:** Exports accounted for 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in the previous year, indicating a positive trend in international market penetration `[Source 3]`.
- **Debt Management:** The company's net debt to equity ratio is a manageable 0.35, reflecting prudent financial management and capacity for future investments `[Source 2]`.

## Bear Case
- **Margin Pressures:** Operating margins are under pressure due to rising copper prices, with a lag in contract price resets affecting profitability. This could impact future earnings if costs cannot be passed on to customers `[Source 1]`, `[Source 2]`.
- **Increased Receivable Days:** The company's receivable days have increased from 78 to 96, primarily due to delayed payments from two large state utility customers, raising concerns about working capital management `[Source 2]`.
- **Regulatory Risks:** Sarvottam Cables has received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore related to input tax credits claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. While the company believes it has a strong case, this could pose a financial risk if the appeal is unsuccessful `[Source 7]`.
- **Promoter Pledging Concerns:** Although the percentage of pledged shares has decreased, the current level of 4.1% of promoter holding remains a concern for minority shareholders, as high pledging can lead to stock price volatility `[Source 8]`.

## Open Questions
- **Clarification on Margin Recovery:** How effectively can the company manage to pass on rising copper costs to customers in the upcoming quarters?
- **Resolution of GST Demand:** What is the timeline for the appeal process regarding the GST demand order, and what are the potential financial implications if the appeal is unsuccessful?
- **Impact of Increased Receivable Days:** What measures is the company implementing to address the increase in receivable days and ensure timely collections from state utilities?
