# Investment Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a leading manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors, catering primarily to power utilities and data centers. In Q1 FY27, the company reported a revenue of ₹1,248 crore, an 18% increase year-on-year, with a net profit of ₹82 crore, reflecting a 5.9% growth. However, the EBITDA margin contracted to 11.2% due to rising copper costs. The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of June 30, 2026, indicating strong demand in the sector.

## Bull Case
- **Strong Revenue Growth:** The company maintains a revenue growth guidance of 15-17% for FY27, supported by demand from power transmission and data center projects, which are expected to contribute significantly to revenue growth in the coming years [Source 2].
- **Capacity Expansion:** The new Bharuch plant, which is set to increase cable capacity by approximately 30%, is on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27, enhancing the company's ability to meet growing demand [Source 3].
- **Improved Export Contribution:** Exports accounted for 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in the previous year, indicating a positive trend in international market penetration [Source 3].
- **Debt Management:** The company has a manageable net debt-to-equity ratio of 0.35, reflecting prudent financial management and capacity to fund expansions through internal accruals [Source 2].

## Bear Case
- **Margin Pressures:** The company faces significant margin pressures due to rising copper prices, which have increased by nearly 14% in the last quarter. The lag in price-reset clauses in contracts may further exacerbate this issue [Source 1].
- **Increased Receivable Days:** Receivable days have risen from 78 to 96, primarily due to delayed payments from large state utility customers, which could impact cash flow and working capital [Source 2].
- **Regulatory Risks:** Sarvottam Cables has received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore, which could pose a financial risk if the appeal does not resolve favorably [Source 7].
- **Promoter Pledging Concerns:** Although the percentage of pledged shares has decreased, it still raises governance concerns, as high promoter pledging can lead to stock price volatility [Source 4].

## Open Questions
- Clarification is needed on the potential impact of the GST demand order on the company's financials and operations [Source 7].
- Further details on the timeline for the Bharuch plant's commercial production and its expected contribution to revenue would be beneficial [Source 3].
- The increase in receivable days warrants monitoring; any defaults from state utilities could significantly affect liquidity [Source 2].
